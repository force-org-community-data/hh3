# Code of Conduct

We want this to be a welcoming place to work on game data.

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Disagree about rules and data, not about people.
- No harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Keep discussion on topic and constructive; give specific, actionable review feedback.
- Credit others' work.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close pull requests, or block participants who break these rules. Report problems to the maintainers listed in `.github/CODEOWNERS` or by opening a private security/contact request on the repository.
