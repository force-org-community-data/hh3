# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Paragon of Metal is only offered to Units made entirely of Automata, as its rule says; Magi, Tech-priests, the Armiger Moirax and vehicles that take the Cybernetica High Tech Arcana were offered it.
- Legion Auxiliary Detachments unlocked by a Command choice (Veteran Cadre, Recon Demi-Company, Storm Battery, Dreadnought Talon, Maelstrom Sentry Battery, Daemonic Manifestation) allow one per unlocking Unit, like the Questoris Detachments, and every variant of the unlocking Unit counts (a Legion Champion with Jump Pack, a Siege Breaker on Scimitar Jetbike, a Mortificator in Tartaros Terminator Armour…); only the plain variant did. Word Bearers add one more Daemonic Manifestation with Erebus. The Hammer of Olympia and Medusan Vanguard requirement texts name their Units.
- The 18 Solar Auxilia Legiones Auxilia designations no longer carry their Advanced Reaction as a special rule (Advanced Reactions are left out of this data; the Legion restriction is each designation’s `when`).
- The core Prime Advantages carry their rules text, and Master Sergeant and Logistical Benefit are limited to once per Detachment (Master Sergeant marks its unit with a Master Sergeant trait for this). The eight Questoris Household Ranks limited to once per Army say so in their text.
- Duplicate records agree across factions: the Solar Auxilia flamer has Template and Panic (1), the Daemons `heavy` rule is the Heavy (X) weapon rule its weapon uses, Custodes, Psykana and Assassinorum Breaching use the current wording, and Frag grenades, Irad-cleanser and Solarite power gauntlet are spelt alike. Multi-profile weapons name their profiles without the weapon name (“Sustained fire”) in every faction, and five unit names lose capitalised small words (Caster of Runes, Magnus the Red, Khârn the Bloody, Maloghurst the Twisted, Praetorian Command Squad with Jump Packs).
- Each Saturnine Chosen in a Saturnine Terminator Command Squad may exchange its paired Saturnine disruption fists on its own, priced per model; it was one pick for the whole unit.
- The Dark Fury Squad’s second Chooser of the Slain is a real model: at 10 Models one Dark Fury may become a Chooser for +5 Points, with the Chooser’s profile, gear and melta bombs option. It was a marker item.
- Each Legion’s trait modifier is the first of its unit modifiers.
- Shattered Legions: a Legion choice whose Units get the Shattered Legions Trait and Mutable Tactics, plus a new "Shattered Legions: Legions" pick of two or three Legions (one sub-faction per Legion). Each pick adds its Mutable Tactics benefit, that Legion's Armoury options (copied from the Legion file, aimed at Shattered Legions Models), an Exemplars of the Legion Prime Advantage that adds a slot for a non-Unique Unit of that Legion, and its Commander of the Shattered Legions (Hibou Khan, Alexis Polux, Shadrak Meduson, Saul Tarvitz, Garviel Loken). Bound in Blood applies when two Legions are picked. Not modelled: one Armoury per Model, and Legion items that live in the Legion's copy of a shared wargear list. The Pride of the Legion, Sky-Hunter, Armoured Spearhead and Orbital Drop rule against slot-adding Prime Advantages counts Exemplars of the Legion.
- Blackshields: a Legion choice for generic Legiones Astartes Units with the Bastard Sons of Fate Tactica and Endryd Haar, plus an "Oaths of Moment" pick (two for a Primary Detachment, one for an Allied one). All fifteen Oaths are picks: Panoply of Old is one pick per Legion carrying that Legion's Armoury; The Spoils of Victory and The Blade of the Just exchange each Unit's Line or Vanguard for Loot the Dead or Doomed Heroics at the value the rule gives; The Flesh is Weak, The Legacy of Nikaea (Sergeant, Champion, Specialist and Command Models gain the Psyker and Malignant Traits and the Malignant Discipline with Warp Torrent), The Broken Helix (Clone or Aberrant) and Alone and Forgotten change rules and Characteristics; In Disgrace all are Equal makes every Slot offer only Petty Warlord; Pride is our Armour, In Disgrace and Alone and Forgotten flag the Choices they forbid; The Weapons of Desperation offers the reclaimed weapons as free exchanges for bolters and bolt pistols; Oaths that exclude each other say so. Not modelled: Infantry becoming Automata, Troops Slots becoming Elites or Command Slots, the Petty Warlord Champion Sub-Type and Auxiliary attachment, and the Taint of the Xenos weapons in the shared lists (their profiles are in the Oath text).
- Knights-Errant: a Loyalist Legion choice holding Nathaniel Garro and Tylos Rubio (with Libertas, Polaris, the Paragon Bolter and Echoes of Fate); other Units are unavailable in it, as its Faction Trait requires.
- Rite of War: Legiones Hereticus: a new "Rite of War" pick on Legiones Astartes, offered to Traitor Emperor's Children and World Eaters, that swaps Flawless Execution for Lords of Profligacy and Violence Incarnate for The Nails' Bite.
- The True Believers (Traitor, non-Vehicle Units gain Malefic, given as a trait since effects can't add a Sub-Type) and Interdiction Cadres (Tactical and Despoiler Squads gain Concealed Positions and Expendable (1)) Prime Advantages.
- Legiones Astartes Daemonic Manifestation Auxiliary Detachment (Heavy Assault slots for Ruinstorm Daemon Brutes), needing a Traitor Esoterist; the Word Bearers version also opens with Erebus (Harbinger of Chaos) in the Primary Detachment.
- Mechanicum Heterodoxists: Cybertheurgists may take the Heterodox Trait (Traitor, not Malagra) and Heterodox Models the Malefic trait, for free, and a list rule flags Renegades, Madmen and Visionaries Units (Draykavac, Scoria, Blood Slaughterer, Brass Scorpion, Decimator, Kytan) unless their Primary or Allied Detachment itself holds a Heterodox Model (one in a linked Auxiliary or Apex Detachment doesn't count).
- Orbital Drop Assault and Sky-Hunter Phalanx check who can actually embark, by count: Walkers against Dreadnought Drop Pods, Dreadclaws and Kharybdis (one each) and Thunderhawk Gunships and Sokar Stormbirds (two each); Vehicles other than Rhinos and Land Raider Carriers or Exploratores are flagged, and those need Thunderhawk Transporters (two Rhinos or one Land Raider) or Sokar Stormbirds (one Rhino). Orbital Drop Assault also counts Units with Bulky Models against Transports without Light Transport (not Drop Pods or Termites) and flags more Drop Pods or Dreadnought Drop Pods than Units (Walkers) to fill them. Transport Capacity and the Bulky (6)/(7) limits are not checked.
- Thousand Sons Prosperine Arcana: every Thousand Sons Unit (not Units with Vehicle, Automata or Unique Models, nor the Khenetai Occult Cabal and Inductii, which have their own) may take one Arcana for +10 Points: Raptora (Crushing Force, Kine Shield), Pyrae (Burning Grasp, Inferno Shield), Pavoni (Bloodboil, Stoneform), Corvidae (Fated Shots, Paths of Consequence), Athanaean (Emanation of Dread, Clarity) or, for Saturnine Praetors, Centurions, Command Squads and Terminator Squads and Walkers, Osiraean (Psychometry, Machine Empathy). Each is equipment carrying its Psychic Weapon profile, its powers are equipment with their rules text, and the Unit gains its Trait. Magnus holds all five Arcana, Ahzek Ahriman the Corvidae Arcana and the Thaumaturgy and Divination Disciplines (with their Special Rules and Traits), the Khenetai Occult Cabal Bladeweaving and Mindsong of Blades, the Inductii Ætheric Guidance, and Zardu Layak Soul Binding and Breach the Veil; those powers moved out of the units’ rule text into equipment.
- Psychic Disciplines give everything their rules list. Each Psychic Power, Psychic Reaction and Gambit that isn't a weapon (Biomantic Rage, Every Strike Foreseen, Foresight’s Blessing, Force Barrier, Mind-burst, Resurrection, Tranquillity, Stormwrought, Call of the Wind, Breach the Veil, Seal the Veil, Dark Channeling) is an equipment record with its rules text, given with its Discipline, and picking a Discipline adds its Special Rule (Impact (Strength), Duellist’s Edge (2), Explodes (4+), Shrouded (4+), Fear (1), Hatred (Psykers), Hatred (Psyker), Move Through Cover) and Trait (Biomancer, Diviner, Pyromancer, Telekine, Telepath, Thaumaturge, Caster of Runes, Stormseer) to the psyker. In the Imperialis Militia and the Daemons of the Ruinstorm these go only to the Rogue Psyker or the Command or Champion model; those already have Fear (1), so Telepathy and Anathemata add no second one. Anathemata (Esoterists, Erebus, Lorgar) and Diabolism (Word Bearers Diabolists) are Discipline equipment instead of special rules: they carry the Void Darts and Hellfire profiles, the holder has Fear (1), and Esoterists hold Breach the Veil and Seal the Veil (Erebus and Lorgar, Traitors, only Breach the Veil). The Militia’s Anathemata gains Void Darts. Tylos Rubio gains Fear (1), Duellist’s Edge (2) and his Telepathy and Divination powers. Impact and Hatred move up to Legiones Astartes (the identical copies in 18 and 11 sub-factions are gone), the Militia gains Explodes and Shrouded and the Daemons of the Ruinstorm Shrouded. The `void-darts` and `hellfire` weapons and the `anathemata-discipline` and `diabolism-discipline` rules are gone; they were default gear and profile rules, so saved lists are unaffected.
- Psychic Disciplines carry their Psychic Weapon profiles (Biomantic Slam, Wildfire, Conflagration, Immovable Force, Cursed Whispers, Wrath of the Death Wolf, Unseen Bolt), and Esoterists hold Void Darts and the Expeditionary Navigator its Lidless Stare. Word Bearers Diabolists gain the Diabolism Discipline (with Hellfire) and Damned. Force moves up to Legiones Astartes, and the Daemons of the Ruinstorm and Solar Auxilia gain the rule records their psychic weapons use.
- Yeomanry Mesnie is one detachment whose slots take Solar Auxilia and Imperialis Militia Units in any combination, as its rule says. Before, it was split into a Solar Auxilia and an Imperialis Militia version that couldn't be mixed. The `yeomanry-mesnie-imperialis-militia` group is gone, so a saved list that used it needs that detachment rebuilt. Each slot's `source` lists both factions, and one set of trait replaces turns either Faction Trait into the Household's.
- Uses validator 2.9.0 (`minValidatorVersion` 2.9.0 in the manifest) for trait effects on some profiles (2.9.0) and slot sources that list several factions (2.8.0). The dev dependency and the doc links point at v2.9.0. This needs a Force Org app with validator 2.9.0 support.
- Unit fixes from a check against every Wahapedia datasheet: the Saturnine Terminator Command Squad may add up to 4 Saturnine Chosen (+60 each) and the Word Bearers Phaeretus Anointed Conclave up to 3 Phaetus Anointed (+65 each), which they couldn't before. Zardu Layak gains his up to 2 Anakatis Kul (+60 each), who now hold the Anakatis blade and plasma pistol he carried himself, and his Hatred (Loyalist) and Binder of Souls rules. Surgeon-Primus Aevos Jovan gains his up to 2 Medicae Orderlies (+10 each), who hold the needle pistols and Triage that Jovan and the Auto-gurney had. The Thousand Sons Numerologist gains Numerologist Order and Battlesmith (2), and the Life Wards their Life Wards rule.
- Every Unique character can be taken once per Army: 48 of them (Legion characters such as Sigismund, Abaddon and Sevatar, and Aevos Jovan) had no Unique limit, only the Primarchs and a few others did.
- Linebreaker Echelon (Cataphractii, Tartaros) accepts the Dark Angels Cataphractii and Tartaros Terminator Deathwing Companions in its Retinue slot, as their Unit Names include the chosen word.
- Legiones Astartes: the five additional Primary Detachments from the 2026 designer's commentary (Pride of the Legion, Fury of the Ancients, Sky-Hunter Phalanx, Armoured Spearhead, Orbital Drop Assault), usable in place of the Crusade Primary Detachment. Pride of the Legion adds one Heavy Transport slot per Retinue, Heavy Assault or Elites unit; Orbital Drop Assault adds one Transport or Heavy Transport slot per non-Vehicle unit. Since the app doesn't build repeating slots yet, these are fixed slots that each open once enough units are taken (13 for Pride, 28 for Orbital Drop, the most either Army can use). The Ancient of War and Skilled Riders Prime Advantages are offered only in their own detachment's Prime Slots, and the detachments' Prime Slots don't offer Prime Advantages that add slots (Fury: only War-engine slots). List rules flag Command/Specialist models without Battlesmith (Fury), non-Vehicle models without Battlesmith and immobile units (Armoured Spearhead), Lords of War without the Transport Sub-Type (Pride, Fury), additional Detachments (Allied included) that a detachment doesn't allow, a missing Master of the Legion, a second First Strike (Orbital Drop), Sky-Hunter Phalanx Units that must begin Embarked on a Flyer Transport outnumbering the Flyer Transports (any Unit with a Model that is neither Antigrav nor a Flyer, Command and High Command Units aside; one Unit per Transport), Orbital Drop Assault Units that must begin Embarked outnumbering the Transports that can carry them (Units without Deep Strike, Orbital Assault Vehicle or the Flyer Sub-Type, Command and High Command Units aside; Transports with the Flyer Sub-Type, Deep Strike or Orbital Assault Vehicle) and slot-adding Prime Advantages picked in other detachments' Prime Slots (Logistical Benefit, Clade/Cult Operative, Rewards of Treachery, Logisticae, The Iron-clad, Paragon of Malevolence). Battlesmith is read with a selector `specialRules` match, so Legion characters with it (Iron Fathers, Warsmiths) count without per-Legion unit lists. Every Vehicle in an Armoured Spearhead Army gains Squadron Tactics (the group's `listEffects`). In Armoured Spearhead, a Lord of War with Decurion Magna may fill the High Command slot and an Armour unit with another Decurion may fill a Command slot (slot `unitWhen`). Only vehicles that can take the Decurion are offered there (with a hint), and one placed without it is flagged; the slots still grant Auxiliary and Apex Points as High Command and Command. In Pride of the Legion, each Unit with Vanguard (X) may exchange it for Line (1): the detachment's slots carry the new Pride of the Legion slot tag, which offers that exchange to Units holding Vanguard (a special-rule `replace`). White Scars may exchange Sky-Hunter Retinue slots for Golden Keshig and Fast Attack slots for Kyzagan Assault Speeders (an exchanged slot isn't Prime); those slots take only Retinue or Fast Attack Units and those two, so later Elites or Support Units stay out.
- The Legiones Decurion upgrades: Decurion Defensor and Decurion Locus (Predator, Sicaran, Kratos), Decurion Magna (Fellblade, Falchion) and the Imperial Fists Decurion Sagittar; a unit takes at most one Decurion, the Sons of Horus Decurion Lanius included. Every Decurion gives its unit the Legiones Decurion trait, which Armoured Spearhead’s Command and High Command slots read (Lanius and Sagittar live in the Legion files).
- Uses validator 2.7.0 (`minValidatorVersion` 2.7.0 in the manifest) for slot `unitWhen`, group `addedSlotRoles` and slot option `when` (2.6.0), group `listEffects` (2.7.0) and selector `specialRules` and special-rule `replace` (2.5.0; the selector also replaces the per-Legion Battlesmith unit lists); the dev dependency and the doc links point at v2.7.0. These fields need a Force Org app with validator 2.7.0 support.
- Eternal Warrior and Shrouded move from the Legion sub-factions to the Legiones Astartes faction (the copies were identical). Logisticae gives its unit a marker trait so list rules can see it; Rewards of Treachery, Paragon of Malevolence and Exemplars of the Legion give theirs to the unit in the slot they add, as Logistical Benefit does, so Fury of the Ancients allows them when that slot is a War-engine slot (and now counts Exemplars of the Legion, which it missed). A rule against slot-adding Prime Advantages flags them once their slot is filled.

- Rules text for the 38 Legion equipment records that had none (Legiones Astartes and 12 Legion sub-factions): the Biomancy, Divination, Pyromancy, Telekinesis, Telepathy, Thaumaturgy, Stormcalling and Runecasting Disciplines, the Icon of Allegiance, strato-vox, machinator array, Proteus pattern storm shield and the Legion characters' relics, among others, taken from Wahapedia. Pyrax missiles and phosphex shells say which weapon profile they unlock.
- Rules text for the other 20 equipment records that had none: the psychic Disciplines in the Imperialis Militia and six of the Ætheric Dominions of the Daemons of the Ruinstorm, and the Solar Auxilia flare launchers, ætherlabe staff and blast-charger (which says which lasrifle profile it unlocks).
- The Dark Angels Inner Circle Knights Cenobium – Order of the Broken Claws hold their plasma-casters as weapons, as the plain Cenobium already did (an empty plasma caster equipment record is gone), and the Night Lords Nostraman chainglaive option gives the chainglaive weapon instead of an empty equipment record (its option id is unchanged, so saved lists still load).
- Frag grenades are equipment, not a weapon, in all six factions that have them: their rules text says the attack may only be made instead of Volley Attacks during the Charge Procedure, and the profile sits on the equipment (`weapon`). Units hold them as equipment, and the Imperialis Militia option that adds them gives equipment (its option id is unchanged, so saved lists still load).
- Uses validator 2.3.0 (`minValidatorVersion` 2.3.0 in the manifest) for equipment with a weapon profile; it is checked with validator 2.4.0, and the dev dependency and the doc links point at v2.4.0.
- Legion wargear options (67 rules across 16 Legions, such as the Dark Angels artificia kill-switch, Sons of Horus Carsoran power axes and Imperial Fists Vigil-pattern storm shield) are no longer offered to Unique characters, as the core Unique Sub-Type rule says a Unique Model may only take the options on its own Army List Profile.
- Uses validator 2.4.0 (`minValidatorVersion` 2.4.0 in the manifest): the Clade Operative Prime Advantage is one slot effect with `count: 3` instead of three copies of it, so it adds all three Support slots; the doc links point at v2.4.0.
- The Clade Operative and Cult Operative Prime Advantages are offered only when the Unit in the Prime Slot has the Loyalist or the Traitor Allegiance respectively (its own trait or the Army's Allegiance), as their rules say; before, any Prime Slot offered both.
- The remaining Wahapedia Army Lists: Imperialis Militia (32 units, the eight wargear lists, nine detachments and the 19 Provenances as sub-factions), Questoris Familia (12 units, the Knight Households Primary Detachment, five Additional Detachments, the ten Household Rank Prime Advantages and the three Household Paradigms as sub-factions), Legio Titanicus (the four Titans, Secutarii and the Titan Ordinal Primary Detachment), Skitarii Conclaves (8 units, with the four Conclave Traits chosen per unit), Daemons of the Ruinstorm (16 units and the eight Ætheric Dominions as sub-factions) and Cults Abominatio (the Infernus Abomination). Battlefield Fortifications has no datasheets on Wahapedia and is not included.
- `game.json`: the Titan unit type and stat template (one profile per part of the Titan), rules text for the Knight unit type, and the Cult Operative Prime Advantage (a Traitor Primary Detachment's Prime Slot adds one Support slot filled from the Cults Abominatio).
- The Lord of War Detachment may be of any Faction, the Army's own included, as the core rules say: its group type is now a root type with `source: "any"` (at most one per Army), so a Legion Army can take a Knight, a Titan or Ka’bandha there. The Titan Ordinal rule that keeps Legio Titanicus Units out of its Lord of War Detachment now reads the main contingent.
- Uses validator 2.2.0 (`minValidatorVersion` 2.2.0 in the manifest): root group types with `source: "any"` (2.1.0), the `slotTag` effect and slot option categories (2.2.0); the doc links point at v2.2.0.
- The ten Household Rank Prime Advantages have the `household-rank` category, and the prime slots of the Knight Households Primary Detachment and the Support Banner offer only those, as their rules say (the generic Prime Advantages no longer show there).
- Knight Household Additional Detachments (Armiger Talon, Automata Talon, Support Banner, Yeomanry Mesnie) have their own group type and link to a Knight Households Primary Detachment or to a Lord of War Detachment, as the Household Paradigms allow.
- Arms of the Omnissiah and Survivors of the Dark Age hold Imperialis Militia copies of the vehicles they let Militia Detachments take (Triaros Armoured Conveyor and Krios Battle Tank; Rhino, Termite and Land Raider Carrier), with the Imperialis Militia Trait, Third-Line and Ballistic Skill -1.

- Exchanges that added the new wargear without removing the old now swap it: Rhino and Damocles Command Rhino combi-bolters, Sokar Stormbird Gravis heavy bolter batteries (each lascannon), Assault Sergeant chainsword and/or bolt pistol and lightning claws, Sanguinary Guard paragon blade, Crimson Paladin shield weapons, Interemptor plasma burner exchanges, Adherent forge-crafted heavy flamer, Atramentar Trucidor headsman’s axe and Fulmentarus Sergeant thunder hammer.
- The Salamanders and Ultramarines Inductii Squads gain the sergeant their datasheets give them (Inductii Master, Evocatus Intendant: 0-1, replacing an Inductii for +15 Points), and their sergeant options sit on that profile.
- Mendicant Knights: with the Questoris Mendicant (Freeblade Company) Household Paradigm, each Questoris Unit in a Lord of War Detachment may take one Household Rank Prime Advantage, as if it filled a Prime Slot.

- Forces of the Emperor: the Legio Custodes (20 units, the five Convocation detachments, the Prefect and Hetaeron Guard Prime Advantages), Anathema Psykana (4 units) and Divisio Assassinorum (7 Assassins) factions.

- The Clade Operative Prime Advantage in `game.json`: any Loyalist Primary Detachment's Prime Slot can add three Support slots filled from the Divisio Assassinorum.

The Stormhammer gains its co-axial multi-laser, and the Dracosan's "Melee weapon" upgrade is renamed "Vehicle equipment".
