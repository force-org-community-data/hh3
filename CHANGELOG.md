# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Rules text for the 38 Legion equipment records that had none (Legiones Astartes and 12 Legion sub-factions): the Biomancy, Divination, Pyromancy, Telekinesis, Telepathy, Thaumaturgy, Stormcalling and Runecasting Disciplines, the Icon of Allegiance, strato-vox, machinator array, Proteus pattern storm shield and the Legion characters' relics, among others, taken from Wahapedia. Pyrax missiles and phosphex shells say which weapon profile they unlock.
- Rules text for the other 20 equipment records that had none: the psychic Disciplines in the Imperialis Militia and six of the Ætheric Dominions of the Daemons of the Ruinstorm, and the Solar Auxilia flare launchers, ætherlabe staff and blast-charger (which says which lasrifle profile it unlocks).
- The Dark Angels Inner Circle Knights Cenobium – Order of the Broken Claws hold their plasma-casters as weapons, as the plain Cenobium already did (an empty plasma caster equipment record is gone), and the Night Lords Nostraman chainglaive option gives the chainglaive weapon instead of an empty equipment record (its option id is unchanged, so saved lists still load).
- Frag grenades are equipment, not a weapon, in all six factions that have them: their rules text says the attack may only be made instead of Volley Attacks during the Charge Procedure, and the profile sits on the equipment (`weapon`). Units hold them as equipment, and the Imperialis Militia option that adds them gives equipment (its option id is unchanged, so saved lists still load).
- Uses validator 2.3.0 (`minValidatorVersion` 2.3.0 in the manifest) for equipment with a weapon profile; it is checked with validator 2.4.0, and the dev dependency and the doc links point at v2.4.0.
- Legion wargear options (67 rules across 16 Legions, such as the Dark Angels artificia kill-switch, Sons of Horus Carsoran power axes and Imperial Fists Vigil-pattern storm shield) are no longer offered to Unique characters, as the core Unique Sub-Type rule says a Unique Model may only take the options on its own Army List Profile.
- The remaining Wahapedia Army Lists: Imperialis Militia (32 units, the eight wargear lists, nine detachments and the 19 Provenances as sub-factions), Questoris Familia (12 units, the Knight Households Primary Detachment, five Additional Detachments, the ten Household Rank Prime Advantages and the three Household Paradigms as sub-factions), Legio Titanicus (the four Titans, Secutarii and the Titan Ordinal Primary Detachment), Skitarii Conclaves (8 units, with the four Conclave Traits chosen per unit), Daemons of the Ruinstorm (16 units and the eight Ætheric Dominions as sub-factions) and Cults Abominatio (the Infernus Abomination). Battlefield Fortifications has no datasheets on Wahapedia and is not included.
- `game.json`: the Titan unit type and stat template (one profile per part of the Titan), rules text for the Knight unit type, and the Cult Operative Prime Advantage (a Traitor Primary Detachment's Prime Slot adds one Support slot filled from the Cults Abominatio).
- The Lord of War Detachment may be of any Faction, the Army's own included, as the core rules say: its group type is now a root type with `source: "any"` (at most one per Army), so a Legion Army can take a Knight, a Titan or Ka’bandha there. The Titan Ordinal rule that keeps Legio Titanicus Units out of its Lord of War Detachment now reads the main contingent.
- Uses validator 2.2.0 (`minValidatorVersion` 2.2.0 in the manifest): root group types with `source: "any"` (2.1.0), the `slotTag` effect and slot option categories (2.2.0); the doc links point at v2.2.0.
- The ten Household Rank Prime Advantages have the `household-rank` category, and the prime slots of the Knight Households Primary Detachment and the Support Banner offer only those, as their rules say (the generic Prime Advantages no longer show there).
- Knight Household Additional Detachments (Armiger Talon, Automata Talon, Support Banner, Yeomanry Mesnie) have their own group type and link to a Knight Households Primary Detachment or to a Lord of War Detachment, as the Household Paradigms allow.
- Arms of the Omnissiah and Survivors of the Dark Age hold Imperialis Militia copies of the vehicles they let Militia Detachments take (Triaros Armoured Conveyor and Krios Battle Tank; Rhino, Termite and Land Raider Carrier), with the Imperialis Militia Trait, Third-Line and Ballistic Skill -1.
- Unused Gambit and Advanced Reaction rules are gone from the Legion files (41 rules), and rules text loses its "LEGION TACTICA" / "GAMBIT" labels and stray spaces before punctuation.
- Salamanders: Blood of Fire, the second rule of their Legion Tactica, now applies with Promethean Endurance.
- Rules text for Thunderstrike (Alpha Legion), Conduit (Thousand Sons), Navigator’s Arts and Rapier Section (Solar Auxilia) and the Anathemata Discipline (Legiones Astartes), which were empty.
- Mechanicum Melee Weapons: the merged "Power weapon" entry is the power sword, power axe, power maul and power lance, each a weapon of its own (the power sword keeps the option id, so saved lists still load). Thousand Sons Æther-fire names are in sentence case.
- Exchanges that added the new wargear without removing the old now swap it: Rhino and Damocles Command Rhino combi-bolters, Sokar Stormbird Gravis heavy bolter batteries (each lascannon), Assault Sergeant chainsword and/or bolt pistol and lightning claws, Sanguinary Guard paragon blade, Crimson Paladin shield weapons, Interemptor plasma burner exchanges, Adherent forge-crafted heavy flamer, Atramentar Trucidor headsman’s axe and Fulmentarus Sergeant thunder hammer.
- The Salamanders and Ultramarines Inductii Squads gain the sergeant their datasheets give them (Inductii Master, Evocatus Intendant: 0-1, replacing an Inductii for +15 Points), and their sergeant options sit on that profile.
- Mendicant Knights: with the Questoris Mendicant (Freeblade Company) Household Paradigm, each Questoris Unit in a Lord of War Detachment may take one Household Rank Prime Advantage, as if it filled a Prime Slot.
- The 25% limit on Warlord and Lord of War points no longer applies to an Army whose Primary Detachment is a Titan Ordinal or Knight Households detachment, as their rules say.
- Forces of the Emperor: the Legio Custodes (20 units, the five Convocation detachments, the Prefect and Hetaeron Guard Prime Advantages), Anathema Psykana (4 units) and Divisio Assassinorum (7 Assassins) factions.
- A list rule flags a Legio Custodes or Anathema Psykana contingent in an Army without the Loyalist Allegiance (their units already had a "Loyalist units only" alert each).
- The Clade Operative Prime Advantage in `game.json`: any Loyalist Primary Detachment's Prime Slot can add three Support slots filled from the Divisio Assassinorum.
- Uses validator 1.11.0: weapons with a ranged and a melee profile (Custodian spears, Galatus warblade, Verutum lance) are one weapon each; the Shield Trait sits on the Sentinel Guard profile only; vehicle weapons show where they are mounted (`defaultGear` `note`, upgrade `gearNote`).
- Mount notes for the Legiones Astartes, Solar Auxilia and Mechanicum vehicles (and the legion vehicles in sub-factions), taken from each datasheet's wargear and option text, and on the Pintle and Sponson weapon lists. The Stormhammer gains its co-axial multi-laser, and the Dracosan's "Melee weapon" upgrade is renamed "Vehicle equipment".
- Units the rules limit to one per Army lose the "0-1" prefix from their names, and a list rule now flags a second one: the Dark Angels Excindio Battle-automata, Blood Angels Ofanim Court (with or without Jump Packs, shared), Emperor's Children Sun Killer Squad, Imperial Fists Huscarl Terminator Retinue, Iron Warriors Dominator Cohort, Thousand Sons Numerologist Cabal, Raven Guard Deliverers Squad, and the Solar Auxilia Davinite Lodge Priest and Expeditionary Navigator.
- The Artificia unit type; the Dark Angels Excindio Battle-automata is Artificia, not Automata.
- Unit types carry their rules text in `description` (from the Wahapedia type tooltips; Vehicle points to the core Vehicle rules).
- Uses validator 1.12.0: the 16 sub-types used as profile tags (Champion, Command, Sergeant, Unique, Light, Heavy, Antigrav, Transport, Flyer, Super-heavy and the rest) are records in `game.json` `subTypes` with their rules text, so a misspelt tag or `tag:` reference now warns.
- Unit traits checked against every Wahapedia datasheet: the 169 Legiones Astartes units gain the Legiones Astartes trait, the Forces of the Emperor units their Loyalist trait and the Traitor Mechanicum units their Traitor trait (their allegiance alerts stay), and traits the page gives one model only (Numerologist Diviner and Telepath, Zardu Layak Psyker) move onto that model's profile.
- Uses validator 1.13.0: the Army's Allegiance gives its trait (Loyalist or Traitor) to every unit in the list that doesn't already carry the other one, as the datasheets' "[Allegiance]" trait says.
- Uses validator 1.14.0, which requires lowercase kebab-case ids: the Assault Squad's Charnabal sabre option id loses its trailing hyphen.
- Uses validator 2.0.0 (schema URLs move to `@2`). It rejects fractional counts and a fixed `min` above a fixed `max`, and adds warnings for unreadable versions, fields set where they are never read, and empty units or upgrades; this data already passed, so no records change.
- Every upgrade on a single-model unit (one model, one profile) is pinned to that profile with `profileId` (578 upgrades on 215 units), and the six sub-faction modifiers that add upgrades only to single-model units (Sons of Horus Decurion Lanius, Iron Hands Gorgon Terminator Armour, Raven Guard Corvid-pattern jump pack, Word Bearers Burning Lore) select `profiles` so their upgrade lands on the profile too.
- The Crusade Primary Detachment is now named "Crusade" (its id, `crusade-primary-detachment`, is unchanged, so saved lists still load).
- A Legion's trait replaces the `[Legiones Astartes]` placeholder trait on the generic Legiones Astartes units instead of being added beside it, as the Legiones Astartes Faction rule says, so those units no longer show both (all 18 Legions).
- Checked with validator 2.4.0 (the dev dependency and the doc links point at v2.4.0; `minValidatorVersion` stays 2.2.0, as this data needs nothing newer).

## 1.0.0

- First tagged release: the Horus Heresy 3rd Edition data as of the data cleanup (legion wargear swaps reachable from their profiles, validator 1.7.0).
